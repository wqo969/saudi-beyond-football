Saudi Beyond Football

A Cultural Journey in Barcelona

ما وراء كرة القدم

A five-day cultural campaign for football fans, students, residents and visitors. Saudi Arabia will host the FIFA World Cup 2034, and Barcelona has both a strong football identity and a rich cultural scene.

Register your interestSee the five days

Vision and objectives

Our vision

Show Saudi Arabia beyond football through a welcoming cultural experience in Barcelona.

What we aim for

Bring football fans, students, residents and visitors into one welcoming cultural space, with free or low-cost registration and information in English and Spanish.

Impact is measured by reach, engagement, clicks and registrations.

Saudi identity and campaign concept

Football brings people together. Culture keeps them connected.

The campaign shares Saudi heritage, food, arts, history and football with people in Barcelona, through five themed days and short bilingual videos.

Five-day event schedule

12:00 – 19:00
